Fixes broken actions — A (X) not confirming chop tree / build / place item (42.22), fliers throwing a Lua error, B closing a menu crouching your character — plus steering, analog pedals, vibration and Steam Input / Steam Deck features. Java mod, needs ZombieBuddy.

description=[h2]Fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]A (X) confirms chop tree, build and place item cursors[/b] (broken in 42.22).
description=[*][b]A picks what you face.[/b] No window behind instead of the sofa in front (A-A still closes the door you ran through); a lamp by the door doesn't toggle with it; A on a furniture / build / chop cursor doesn't also sit down or open a window; the loot window opens on the container in front; half RT or the Steam Aim action no longer turns the character.
description=[*][b]Fliers and brochures work with a controller.[/b] Vanilla threw a Lua error. Fliers and stash maps on the world map now open with A under the cross (vanilla needed the mouse).
description=[*][b]X menu sees fences and walls south and east[/b]: disassemble chain-link and metal fences from any side.
description=[*][b]UI presses don't leak to the character.[/b] Closing a menu with B no longer makes your character crouch on release.
description=[*][b]Cruise control and reload follow your bindings.[/b] Vanilla shows the prompts on the bound buttons but still toggles cruise control on X and opens the reload radial on RB. Now rebinding them in Options -> Controller works.
description=[*][b]Controller presets save in any language.[/b] On Linux and Steam Deck a new preset in a non-English game failed to save.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Improvements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Steering without dead zones.[/b] Driving uses the raw left stick X: no stick dead zone, no diagonal stretch, no 0.1 wheel dead zone.
description=[*][b]No more driving in arcs (stick drift fix).[/b] The mod learns where a released stick rests, so the car goes straight. No dead zone needed.
description=[*][b]Speed-sensitive steering.[/b] Soft center at high speed, full stick = max wheel angle for the speed. No more oversteering on the highway.
description=[*][b]Wheel without lag.[/b] The wheel follows the stick at a constant rate (vanilla lags up to ~3 s at top speed). Keyboard steering stays vanilla.
description=[*][b]Analog gas and brake.[/b] Vanilla LT/RT (L2/R2) are on/off at 70%. Now a light press rolls the car slowly and holds that speed, full pedal = vanilla, no trigger dead zone. Optional: LT brakes to a stop without backing up.
description=[*][b]Easy driving (splitscreen).[/b] Per player: stick = where to go on screen, triggers = pedals. For a partner new to driving. Servers can forbid it.
description=[*][b]Smooth map controls.[/b] World and paper maps pan smoothly by stick deflection, triggers zoom by how far you press.
description=[*][b]Rebindable hardcoded buttons.[/b] The D-pad wheels (vehicle, emotes, weapon/light, zones), the Back wheel, Start, cruise control (X + D-pad), L3 radial and RB-hold UI navigation are hardcoded in vanilla. Each is now an action: rebind or turn it off. Defaults stay vanilla.
description=[*][b]Keyboard-only actions on the controller.[/b] Flashlight, drop items or bag, grab corpse, sharpen, firearm menu, sit, search mode, shout, let go of rope, engine, headlights, heater, horn, switch seat, vehicle info, map, crafting, building, skills, health, hotbar slots 1-8, game speed, move furniture, hide UI, screenshot, PvP, push-to-talk, music, camera pan, animal radial, Shift actions (driver's seat, curtains, fire mode), use tool ahead (chop, dig, water). Unbound by default. Per splitscreen player.
description=[*][b]Steam Input game actions.[/b] All mod actions appear in the Steam controller configurator: back paddles, long/double press, chords, context layers (one paddle = flashlight on foot, horn in a car). Per player in splitscreen.
description=[*][b]Gyro aiming.[/b] Bind gyro (or a trackpad) to the Aim analog action in Steam: it moves the reticle with a firearm in hand. Works with half trigger alone. Per player.
description=[*][b]Sticks for walking and driving, gas on any trigger.[/b] Move / Steer / Steer + pedals analog actions in Steam: a stick or trackpad drives straight past the game's stick handling. Gas and Brake for any trigger.
description=[*][b]Inventory actions for the controller.[/b] One click like the mouse: Transfer All / Loot All, take same type, move to floor, drop, transfer stack or one item, use (equip, wear, eat), unpack. Unbound by default.
description=[*][b]Vanilla bindings in Steam too.[/b] Crouch, Interact, Sprint, Aim, Attack, Melee, Run, Cancel, Reload, Inventory, Brakes, Cruise control are Steam actions as well: back paddles can crouch, brakes follow their binding.
description=[*][b]Controller vibration (rumble).[/b] Via Steam Input, with per-event and per-player settings. Feel the ready prompt, shots, hits, bites, crashes, curbs and parking stops, suspension, rough roads, falls, tools, burns, glass, zombies at the door, jump scares, thunder, the power outage blast, and shivering from hypothermia. Heartbeat, coughs and sneezes follow their sounds.
description=[*][b]Actions on press, not on release.[/b] Crouch (sneak) toggle and toggle-to-sprint (run) react on button press, no input lag; entering a vehicle only via the A prompt.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Options -> Mods -> Gamepad Tweaks[/b]: toggle features and tune steering, wheel speed, map controls, Steam Input and vibration. Changes apply during play.
description=Options -> Controller: assign mod actions per preset and layer. Action icons match Steam Input. The same button can toggle the flashlight on foot and honk in a car.
description=Advanced: ~/Zomboid/GamepadTweaks.properties.

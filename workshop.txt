title=Gamepad Tweaks
description=Java mod (ZombieBuddy) that fixes gamepad / controller handling in Build 42: Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock, DualSense), Steam Deck, Steam Input and other joysticks.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Steering without dead zones.[/b] Driving a vehicle with a controller uses the raw left stick X: no stick dead zone (deadzone), no diagonal stretch, no 0.1 wheel dead zone. Precise, analog car steering.
description=[*][b]No more driving in arcs (stick drift fix).[/b] A released stick rarely rests at exactly zero, and that tiny offset slowly pulls the car to one side. While the stick stands still near the center, the mod glides the center to where it rests, so the car goes straight without you holding the stick off-center. No dead zone: fine steering stays intact.
description=[*][b]Speed-sensitive steering.[/b] Soft center at high speed, full stick = maximum wheel angle allowed at the current speed. No more oversteering on the highway.
description=[*][b]Wheel without lag.[/b] The steering wheel follows the stick at a constant rate instead of a slow exponential (up to ~3 s steering delay at top speed). Keyboard steering stays vanilla.
description=[*][b]Triggers without dead zone.[/b] LT/RT (L2/R2) use the raw analog trigger axis: gas and brake respond from the first millimeter. The options screen dead zone still applies to the sticks.
description=[*][b]Smooth map controls.[/b] World map and paper maps: past the game's stick dead zone the map pans smoothly, stick deflection = scroll speed (also in isometric view, diagonals work, no jumps). Triggers zoom smoothly, how far you press = zoom speed, instead of one zoom step per click. The paper map can now be moved with the stick too.
description=[*][b]Fliers and brochures work with a controller.[/b] Vanilla throws a Lua error when giving the flier window the gamepad focus, so B (close), X (text) and Y (show on map) did nothing and it closed only with Esc on the keyboard. Fixed, plus a B prompt.
description=[*][b]UI presses don't leak to the character.[/b] Closing a menu with B no longer makes your character crouch etc. when B is released.
description=[*][b]Rebindable hardcoded buttons.[/b] The D-pad wheels (vehicle, emotes, weapon/light, zones/guide), the Back command wheel, Start pause, cruise control speed (X + D-pad), the L3 crouch/sit radial and RB-hold UI navigation are hardcoded in vanilla. Each is now an action you can move to another button or turn off to free the button for regular bindings. Defaults stay vanilla.
description=[*][b]Keyboard-only actions on the controller.[/b] Light source / flashlight toggle, drop held items or bag, grab corpse, sharpen weapon, sit on ground, search mode, shout, let go of rope, engine, headlights, heater, horn, switch seat, vehicle info; extra: map, crafting, building, skills, health, protection, info, survival guide, hotbar slots 1-8, game speed, move furniture, hide UI, screenshot, PvP. Unbound by default, assign any button. Work for every splitscreen player.
description=[*][b]All of it in Options -> Controller.[/b] The mod actions show up in the vanilla controller bindings editor next to the regular bindings: add them to any button with "< Add New >", replace or remove them there.
description=[*][b]Steam Input game actions.[/b] All mod actions (horn, headlights, light source, drop items, wheels, hotbar, map, game speed...) appear in the Steam controller configurator as game actions: put them on back paddles / extra buttons, long press, double press or button chords. Context layers (on foot, in a vehicle, aiming, panel open, map, main/pause menu): one paddle can be the flashlight on foot and the horn in a car, gyro only while aiming or in menus. Sticks and buttons keep working as a gamepad. Can be turned off in the mod options.
description=[*][b]Cruise control and reload follow your bindings.[/b] Vanilla shows the prompts on the bound buttons but still toggles cruise control on X and opens the reload radial on RB. Now rebinding them in Options -> Controller works.
description=[*][b]Actions on press, not on release.[/b] Crouch (sneak) toggle and toggle-to-sprint (run) react on button press, no input lag; entering a vehicle only via the A prompt.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Options -> Mods -> Gamepad Tweaks[/b]: turn off any feature you don't want (steering without dead zone, stick center learning, wheel without lag, triggers without dead zone, crouch/sprint on press, smooth map, Steam Input) and tune it for your controller: steering dead zone, steering curve, wheel speed, map pan and zoom speed. Changes apply at once, in game too. Bug fixes always stay on.
description=Advanced: ~/Zomboid/GamepadTweaks.properties (same settings and more, re-read while the game runs). Button actions: Options -> Controller.

title=Gamepad Tweaks
description=Java mod (ZombieBuddy) that fixes gamepad / controller handling in Build 42: Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock, DualSense), Steam Deck, Steam Input and other joysticks.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Steering without dead zones.[/b] Driving a vehicle with a controller uses the raw left stick X: no stick dead zone (deadzone), no diagonal stretch, no 0.1 wheel dead zone. Precise, analog car steering.
description=[*][b]No more driving in arcs (stick drift fix).[/b] A released stick rarely rests at exactly zero, and the offset slowly pulls the car aside. The mod learns where the stick rests, so the car goes straight. No dead zone needed.
description=[*][b]Speed-sensitive steering.[/b] Soft center at high speed, full stick = maximum wheel angle allowed at the current speed. No more oversteering on the highway.
description=[*][b]Wheel without lag.[/b] The steering wheel follows the stick at a constant rate instead of a slow exponential (up to ~3 s steering delay at top speed). Keyboard steering stays vanilla.
description=[*][b]Triggers without dead zone.[/b] LT/RT (L2/R2) use the raw analog trigger axis: gas and brake respond from the first millimeter. The options screen dead zone still applies to the sticks.
description=[*][b]Smooth map controls.[/b] World map and paper maps: past the game's stick dead zone the map pans smoothly, stick deflection = scroll speed (also in isometric view, diagonals work, no jumps). Triggers zoom smoothly, how far you press = zoom speed, instead of one zoom step per click. The paper map can now be moved with the stick too.
description=[*][b]Fliers and brochures work with a controller.[/b] Vanilla threw a Lua error, so B, X and Y did nothing and the window closed only with Esc. Fixed. Fliers and stash maps on the world map now open with A under the cross (vanilla needed the mouse).
description=[*][b]Controller presets save in any language.[/b] On Linux and Steam Deck a new controller preset in a non-English game (e.g. "Пользовательский") failed to save, so your bindings were lost. New presets are now named "Custom".
description=[*][b]UI presses don't leak to the character.[/b] Closing a menu with B no longer makes your character crouch etc. when B is released.
description=[*][b]Rebindable hardcoded buttons.[/b] The D-pad wheels (vehicle, emotes, weapon/light, zones/guide), the Back command wheel, Start pause, cruise control speed (X + D-pad), the L3 crouch/sit radial and RB-hold UI navigation are hardcoded in vanilla. Each is now an action you can move to another button or turn off to free the button for regular bindings. Defaults stay vanilla.
description=[*][b]Keyboard-only actions on the controller.[/b] Light source / flashlight toggle, drop held items or bag, grab corpse, sharpen weapon, firearm menu (magazine, rounds, rack; vanilla only on a held reload button), sit on ground, search mode, shout, let go of rope, engine, headlights, heater, horn, switch seat, vehicle info; extra: map, crafting, building, skills, health, protection, info, survival guide, hotbar slots 1-8, game speed, move furniture, hide UI, screenshot, PvP, rotate building, voice push-to-talk, music toggle, FPS graph; camera pan while aiming ("look far", vanilla only on a hardcoded RB: now RB by default and movable, follows the gyro too), animal radial menu; what the keyboard does with Shift: get in the driver's seat, open/close curtains, change fire mode; use the tool in hand on the square ahead (chop, dig, water...). Unbound by default, assign any button. Work for every splitscreen player.
description=[*][b]All of it in Options -> Controller.[/b] Mod actions sit next to the regular bindings: add them to any button with "< Add New >". Saved per controller preset.
description=[*][b]Steam Input game actions.[/b] All mod actions (horn, headlights, light source, drop items, wheels, hotbar, map, game speed...) appear in the Steam controller configurator as game actions: put them on back paddles / extra buttons, long press, double press or button chords. Context layers (on foot, in a vehicle, aiming, panel open, inventory, map, main/pause menu): one paddle can be the flashlight on foot and the horn in a car, gyro only while aiming or in menus. Sticks and buttons keep working as a gamepad. Per player in splitscreen (Linux and Windows). Can be turned off in the mod options.
description=[*][b]Gyro aiming.[/b] Bind gyro (or a trackpad) to the "Aim" analog action in Steam: it moves the aiming reticle on top of the stick, for firearms and melee direction. Works with half trigger (precise aim) alone, no stick needed. A held "Aim" action aims without the stick, like the keyboard key. Activation, sensitivity and dead zones are set in Steam.
description=[*][b]Stick straight on walking and steering.[/b] "Move" and "Steer" analog actions in Steam: bind a stick or trackpad to them in your layout and it drives the character or the car directly, past the game's own left stick handling. Running at full deflection works too.
description=[*][b]Inventory actions for the controller.[/b] What the mouse does in one click: Transfer All / Loot All, transfer or take same type, same type to nearby containers, move everything to the floor, drop the selected item, transfer the whole stack or one item, use (double click: equip, wear, eat), unpack. In vanilla most of them aren't in the gamepad menu at all. Unbound by default: assign them in Options -> Controller or Steam, they work while the inventory / loot window is focused (own Steam layer).
description=[*][b]Vanilla bindings in Steam too.[/b] Crouch, Interact, Sprint, Aim, Attack, Melee (shove), Run, Cancel, Reload, Inventory, Close panel, Brakes, Cruise control... are Steam game actions as well (attack, shove and run on any button, not only trigger and stick thresholds). They run the game action directly, without pressing any button: a back paddle can crouch even with Crouch unbound in the game, and nothing else on that button fires. Your Steam layout stays the same on any PC. Brakes now follow their binding instead of B.
description=[*][b]Cruise control and reload follow your bindings.[/b] Vanilla shows the prompts on the bound buttons but still toggles cruise control on X and opens the reload radial on RB. Now rebinding them in Options -> Controller works.
description=[*][b]Actions on press, not on release.[/b] Crouch (sneak) toggle and toggle-to-sprint (run) react on button press, no input lag; entering a vehicle only via the A prompt.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Options -> Mods -> Gamepad Tweaks[/b]: turn off any feature you don't want (steering without dead zone, stick center learning, wheel without lag, triggers without dead zone, crouch/sprint on press, smooth map, Steam Input) and tune it for your controller: steering dead zone, steering curve, wheel speed, map pan and zoom speed. Changes apply at once, in game too. Bug fixes always stay on.
description=Advanced: ~/Zomboid/GamepadTweaks.properties (same settings and more, re-read while the game runs). Button actions: Options -> Controller.
